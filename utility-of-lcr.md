# Utility of LCR

LCR is a utility token. Its only purpose is to be used inside the Lucrar project. It gives no right to income, dividends or interest, no vote in the company, no share of any asset, and no promise that its value will rise.

> Do not buy the token unless you use it. That sentence is on the homepage on purpose.

## Everything LCR does today (September 2026)

**Reward of the game and of the members area.** Members of the Patreon plans earn LCR by playing Aldeia Lucrar, up to 40 LCR per member per month, and by taking part in the monthly challenge, whose prize goes from 10 to 200 LCR depending on the plan. The LCR earned is credited to the member's balance in the members area, with a statement of every movement. The game itself never touches a wallet: no transaction is sent from it, and nobody needs to own LCR to play.

**Access to off-plan real estate projects.** Anyone holding at least 1,000 LCR can open the monthly folder of off-plan developments ("Projetos em planta") on lucrar.pt. Since September 2025 the company has published 58 teasers, in PDF, one folder per month, selected by its asset analysis team. The tokens are not spent or transferred: the wallet is connected and only the balance is read.

**Access to the company's transaction register.** Anyone holding at least 10 LCR can open the public register of every company movement with the token: the initial liquidity and each purchase of LCR by the company, with a link to each transaction on BscScan.

**Tools.** The tools area of the website (calculators, comparisons, simulators) is part of the platform the token opens.

**Events.** LCR and the project's NFTs were the ticket to the 2023 financial education event. Future events follow the same model.

**Payments to contributors.** Writers who publish approved articles on the website are compensated in LCR.

**Lucrar Pass NFTs.** The 2022 collection was minted with LCR. Current holders are being given new uses inside the platform (see Lucrar Pass NFTs).

## How to get LCR

* **By using the platform.** Members earn LCR in the game and in the monthly challenges. This is the way the project prefers: LCR should reach the people who use it.
* **PancakeSwap** (BNB Smart Chain), pair LCR/WBNB. This is the only market. The company has never listed the token on a centralised exchange.
* **The swap on lucrar.pt.** The homepage has a non-custodial swap window that trades BNB for LCR and LCR for BNB directly through PancakeSwap, with your own wallet. Anyone without BNB can buy it with a card through an on-ramp partner (Onramper) inside the same window; the partner sells the BNB, not the company.
* **The company never sells LCR** from its main wallet. It has never sold a token since the project began.

## The company and the token

The company holds LCR in public cold wallets, adds liquidity, and has bought LCR on the open market twice (4 December 2025 and 27 February 2026, EUR 1,000 each). Each movement is listed in the transaction register with its BscScan link. These are decisions of the company about its own money, disclosed after the fact. They are not a right of LCR holders, they are not an obligation, and they can stop. The CMVM analysis of April 2022 (see Regulation) was based precisely on LCR having no right to income and no obligation on the issuer to act to raise its price. The company keeps it that way.

## What LCR does not do

* No staking, no deposits with interest, no burn, no mint. All of these existed in the 2021 beta and were removed before launch to comply with the CMVM analysis. The economy described in Tokenomics does not bring any of them back: the larger share of any LCR spent on the platform will go to a public address nobody holds the key to. That is not a burn: the token has no burn function and the total supply stays at 100,000,000. What falls is the supply still in circulation.
* No NFT marketplace. The company's own marketplace, planned in 2022, was never built. The NFT collection is closed (see Lucrar Pass NFTs).
* No share of any property or rent. Holding LCR gives no participation in the company's housing or in its results.
