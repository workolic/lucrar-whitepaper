# How it all connects

The project has several doors: the free content, the game, the members area on Patreon, the courses, the investor area and the token. They are one platform, not separate products. This page explains how they fit together.

## The portal: one entrance

[lucrar.pt/portal](https://lucrar.pt/portal) is the single entrance to everything reserved. It asks one question, "where do you want to go?", and offers four doors:

* **I am on Patreon**: the members area, with the materials and benefits of the member's plan.
* **I bought a course**: the courses bought, opened with the buyer's email.
* **I am an exclusive investor**: the private investor area, for the named investors who have contracts with the company (see Real estate and rents). It is not open to the public and it has nothing to do with the token.
* **I am on the Workolic team**: the internal dashboard of the team.

The members area and the courses are opened with an email address and a one-time code sent to it. The private investor area is opened differently: the investor types their mobile number and receives a six-digit code by SMS, with no password at all. No wallet is involved in any of the doors.

## The members area (Patreon)

The Patreon has three paid plans. The free tier is everything public: the YouTube channel and the open pages of the website. Each plan unlocks a cumulative set of resources, so a member of the third plan sees everything the first and second include:

* **Tools**: files, spreadsheets, calculators and private groups (a discussion group and a real estate group) included in the plan.
* **How to start**: a learning path by levels, from organising money to investing to choosing a strategy, step by step.
* **Monthly challenge**: once a month the member submits a screenshot with a short explanation of what it shows; the winner of the month receives a prize in LCR, from 10 to 200 LCR depending on the plan.

The members area is gamified. Opening a step of the path, using a tool for the first time and coming back every day give points and levels, from "Curious" upwards. The member's character from Aldeia Lucrar appears in the members area, and the "Play in the village" button opens the game already signed in.

## Where the token enters

The members area keeps an **LCR balance** for each member. It grows in two ways: by playing Aldeia Lucrar through the members area, up to a monthly cap ("the maximum the village gives this month"), and by winning the monthly challenge. Every movement is listed in a statement. This is how most people in the project come to hold LCR: by using the platform, not by buying.

Holding LCR in a wallet, in turn, opens the parts of the website that are gated by balance: the monthly folder of off-plan real estate projects (1,000 LCR) and the company's transaction register (10 LCR). The wallet is read, never charged.

## The courses

Courses are paid once and hosted on lucrar.pt, with lessons and topics inside the platform. The first is "AI applied to advanced investing", with a waiting list at a discount before it opens. Buying a course does not require LCR. Holders of Lucrar Pass NFTs are being given discounts on courses and on the members area (see Lucrar Pass NFTs).

## The content and the company

The free content brings people in: the videos, the articles, the calculators and the game. The members area and the courses are how the platform pays for its teaching. The company's own results, which come mainly from the rents of the housing it operates, are published every quarter and allocated to rubrics that include the financial education fund. The token connects the pieces: it is earned by learning, and it opens what the company publishes for the people who use the platform.

Nothing in this loop gives LCR a right to any of it. The rents belong to the company, the results are the company's, and the token is a key, not a share.
