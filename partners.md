# Partners

Who helps bring value to the project.

* **JLL**, global real-estate services and investment management company. JLL sends the project off-plan opportunities. The first apartment bought off-plan (Setúbal, completed July 2025) was promoted by JLL.
* **Casas do Tejo**, construction supervision.

## Token and infrastructure

* **PancakeSwap**, the decentralised exchange on BNB Smart Chain where the only LCR market exists.
* **Onramper**, the on-ramp partner integrated in the swap on lucrar.pt so that anyone can buy BNB with a card before swapping it for LCR. Onramper and its payment providers sell the BNB; the company never sells LCR.
* **Fairyproof**, the security firm that audited the LCR smart contract in October 2022.

## Storage

* **Elementum**, gold and silver storage (Swiss vaults).
* **Ledger**, cryptocurrency cold storage.
