# Real estate and rents

The company earns its recurring income from housing. This is the passive income the platform is built on: rents come in every month, the company publishes what it earns, and the results fund the mission and the platform. Every project has a public page on lucrar.pt with dates and amounts as they happen.

## The T46 building: 40 rooms, rented

The company's main asset in operation is a residential building, type T46, with 40 rooms let to tenants. The rents from those 40 rooms are the company's largest source of income. They go into the company's results, together with the members area, the courses and the other activities, and they appear in the quarterly financial statements published on the homepage from December 2026: balance sheet, income statement and cash-flow statement.

What the rents fund is what the company publishes: common expenses, the financial education fund (10% of results), the reserve, research and development, the platform, marketing and cash. The allocation is decided by the company, published after the fact, and can change. It is not a promise to anyone and it is not attached to the token.

## Off-plan projects for LCR holders

Since September 2025 the company publishes a monthly folder of off-plan developments across Portugal (Lisbon, Porto, Algarve, Cascais, Oeiras, Gaia and others), selected by its asset analysis team and shared as PDF teasers. Fifty-eight projects have been published in twelve months. The folder opens for anyone holding 1,000 LCR. This is information, not advice, and the company earns nothing from the developers for publishing it.

## Financing and the token

Some operations are financed by the company itself and some through private contracts with individual investors, handled in the investor area of the portal with its own login. Those contracts are private, between the company and each named investor, and are not described here.

LCR has nothing to do with them. Holding LCR gives no share of any property, no share of any rent, and no participation in any financing. This separation is what keeps LCR a utility token under the CMVM analysis of 2022.
