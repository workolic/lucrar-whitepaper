# Changes since launch

Every material change to the project, the token or the company, in date order. The original 2022 plan was removed from this whitepaper in version 3; this page is the record of what was planned and what was changed.

## Before launch (2021 to April 2022)

* Company renamed from Gustavo Araújo, Lda. to Cripteracia, Unipessoal, Lda., with new corporate purpose, CAE and share capital.
* Removed from the token, to comply with the CMVM analysis: burn, mint, deposits with a fixed 10% annual interest, distribution of dividends in crypto, share capital in the form of cryptocurrency.
* Net income no longer allocated directly to the token; it goes to retained results and is distributed by rubrics.
* Services dropped before launch: consulting of a kind that constitutes investment advice, custody, exchange, and buying LCR directly with a card from the company.
* 2 February 2022: LCR created. 15 March 2022: liquidity added on PancakeSwap (81.8 BNB). 15 April 2022: official launch. 22 April 2022: Lucrar Pass NFT blindbox opened.

## 2022

* 8 May: some early contributors sold part of their tokens after the team asked everyone to lock. The rest of their tokens, 124,604 LCR, was locked permanently. Bank and exchange records were published.
* 26 May: founder, team and contributor wallets above 50,000 LCR (1,537,292 LCR in total) locked for six months, then released at 5% per month.
* 15 May: the company's liquidity on PancakeSwap locked with Mudra for two years, until 15 May 2024.
* 4 August: every quarterly release of 1,000,000 LCR from the contract goes to a permanent lock instead of the market.
* October: Fairyproof audit report published.

## 2023

* Financial education event for NFT holders. Every Lucrar Pass, of any type, gave free access; holders also received LCR and the courses. Ticket money from non-holders was sent to holders of Special passes.
* NFT primary sale closed for good.
* Setúbal: promissory contract for the first apartment (24 January).

## 2024

* Setúbal: works started (29 January).
* The Mudra lock on the company's liquidity expired (15 May). The liquidity stayed in the locker.
* MiCA became applicable in the EU (30 June for asset-referenced and e-money tokens, 30 December for the rest).

## 2025

* 5 April: project update published with a reference price of EUR 0.14.
* July: land for six apartments bought in Montijo (18 July) and the multidisciplinary firm contracted (28 July).
* 30 July: Setúbal apartment completed and fully paid.
* September: monthly folder of off-plan projects starts, for holders of 1,000 LCR.
* 4 December: first purchase of LCR by the company (EUR 1,000).
* 3 December: whitepaper version 2.0 published.

## 2026

* 27 February: second purchase of LCR by the company (EUR 1,000).
* Founder registered with the CMVM as independent financial analyst; member no. 677 of APAF; Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe, Finance.
* Aldeia Lucrar, the village game, launched in place of the 2022 quiz; access portal, members area on Patreon (LCR balance, monthly challenges) and the first course.
* New homepage with the swap in both directions and the card on-ramp; new team and about pages.
* 31 August: editorial policy published.
* 16 September: the PancakeSwap liquidity was withdrawn from the Mudra vault, whose term had ended on 15 May 2024, and locked with UNCX until 14 September 2028. UNCX charged 1% in liquidity tokens, about USD 3,100. The pool reserves and the main wallet balance did not change.
* 12 September: whitepaper version 3 (this document). The 2022 plan pages were removed; on-chain status added; the game, the portal and the rents added; roadmap rewritten to what is actually scheduled; the team page replaced by lucrar.pt/team.
* 13 September: tokenomics rewritten with the on-chain economy the project is building (one payment contract, a lock that cannot pay out, the Aldeia pass, the shop floor and the 50% rule). None of it is on the main network; the token itself is unchanged.
* 15 September: the reserve was converted from BUSD to USDT. BUSD had been discontinued by its issuer and no longer had a reliable market, which made the reserve unusable for its purpose; 585 BUSD became 580.18 USDT. The rule that governs the reserve did not change.
* 16 September: the wording of the payment split was corrected in this whitepaper. Earlier text described the 70% share as going to a lock contract; on the main network it goes to `0x…dEaD`, a public address nobody holds the key to, and it was never a burn. The 10% share was described as the tournament pot; on the main network it is set to the financial education fund wallet. `LcrCaixa` and `PasseDaAldeia` were also described as not yet on the main network; both are published and verified there, and neither has been used.
* 17 September: the village was rebuilt around a central courtyard, and the statuses became five cards, one per door into the project (King, Investor, Joker, Jack, Queen), appearance only and with no effect on play. A counter in the Mercado exchanges LCR for discount codes on the Patreon plan, over the same balance as the members area, in seven tiers from 40 to 900 LCR; the counter is connected but its window is empty until codes are loaded. Progress made without an email is now merged into the account instead of being lost. Nothing changed in `LcrCaixa` or in the liquidity lock.
* December (planned): first quarterly financial statements.
