# Transparency

The project's position is simple: publish the numbers and let people check them. What follows is what is published, where, and how often.

## Quarterly financial statements

From December 2026 the company publishes, every quarter, its balance sheet, income statement and cash-flow statement on the homepage of lucrar.pt. This is where the rents of the 40 rooms, the members area, the courses and every other source of income show up, and where anyone can see how the results were allocated. The first set is due in December 2026. Until then the homepage shows the three documents as "to be disclosed" with the next date.

## Quarterly notice to investors

Every three months, on 15 March, June, September and December, each CAEP investor receives a message on their mobile with what they have accrued to that date, the date of the next annual payment and how many days are left, plus the reminder that the company accounts are published every quarter. Anyone who does not want it switches the notice off in the portal, without having to ask anyone.

## Transaction register

Every movement of the company with LCR is listed on [lucrar.pt/pt/transactions](https://lucrar.pt/pt/transactions/), with the date, the amount and the link to the transaction on BscScan: the initial liquidity of 15 March 2022 (81.8 BNB) and each purchase of LCR by the company (4 December 2025 and 27 February 2026, EUR 1,000 each). The page opens for anyone holding 10 LCR.

## Wallets

The company's four cold wallets are public and named on the company information page and in the Security and protection page of this whitepaper. Anyone can check their balances on BscScan at any time. The on-chain state as of September 2026 is summarised in the Tokenomics section.

## Real estate

Each housing project has a page with the timeline of contracts, payments and works, updated as they happen: [lucrar.pt/pt/setubal](https://lucrar.pt/pt/setubal/) and [lucrar.pt/pt/pegoes](https://lucrar.pt/pt/pegoes/).

## Editorial policy

The website publishes how it earns money and how it checks numbers: [lucrar.pt/politica-editorial](https://lucrar.pt/politica-editorial/). In short: every figure carries a date and a source; sensitive data is confirmed in two independent sources; the only affiliate relationship is disclosed; no broker pays to appear or to rank; errors are corrected and the correction is marked, not hidden.
