# Regulation

Three regulators matter for this project: the CMVM (securities), the Bank of Portugal (virtual asset service providers and anti-money-laundering) and, since 2024, the European MiCA regulation. This page records what each one said or requires, and what the company did.

> Lucrar tokens and NFTs are not securities. Transactions on them are not subject to the rules applicable to the protection of investors in securities.

## CMVM (Portuguese Securities Market Commission)

On 5 April 2022, before launch, the CMVM analysed the project and the token. Its reasoning: a token is a security only if it represents legal situations of a private and patrimonial nature comparable to typical securities (shares, bonds). What makes a token comparable is a right to income, or an obligation on the issuer to act in a way that raises the token's value.

So the company removed, before launch:

* the possibility of depositing tokens and receiving annual interest;
* the burn function;
* the distribution of periodic income to token holders;
* any reference to "shareholders" in the token distribution, since LCR does not represent the share capital.

The CMVM's conclusion was that LCR is not a security, and the same conclusion extends to the project's NFTs. The CMVM also asked that the whitepaper state this prominently, which it does.

The company keeps this line. The purchases of LCR the company has made are decisions about its own money, disclosed after the fact, and not an obligation or a right of holders.

The 2022 analysis was made on the token as it is, and the token has not changed: no mint, no burn, no interest, no income for holders, and the same 100,000,000 supply. The contracts described in Tokenomics sit beside the token, not inside it. They do not add any of those functions and they do not create a right to income. The share of each payment that stops circulating is transferred to a public address nobody holds the key to. It is not destroyed and the total supply does not change; what falls is the supply still able to move. The company will take legal advice on this economy, and on selling courses in LCR, before any of it runs on the main network.

## Bank of Portugal

The company restructured the project before launch so as not to provide custody, exchange, transfer or similar services with virtual assets. It still sent, on 15 October 2021 and 4 March 2022, the communication under Notice no. 3/2021 concerning registration of entities carrying out activities with virtual assets (article 112-A of Law no. 83/2017 of 18 August, as amended by Law no. 58/2020).

The Bank of Portugal replied that the project, as described, did not require prior registration with the Bank under article 112-A. The letter is published on the company information page.

That remains the position: the company holds nobody's tokens, runs no exchange and makes no transfers on behalf of anyone. The swap on lucrar.pt is a non-custodial interface to PancakeSwap: the user's own wallet signs every transaction. The card on-ramp inside the swap is operated by Onramper and its licensed payment providers, who sell BNB to the user; the company is not a party to that sale.

## MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation, EU 2023/1114)

The 2022 version of this whitepaper described MiCA as a proposal. It is now law. MiCA applied from 30 June 2024 for asset-referenced and e-money tokens, and in full from 30 December 2024 for other crypto-assets and for crypto-asset service providers. In Portugal the competent authorities are the CMVM and the Bank of Portugal.

How it touches this project:

* LCR is neither an asset-referenced token nor an e-money token.
* The company does not offer LCR to the public: it has never sold a token and has no offer open. It is not seeking admission to trading on any trading platform; the only market is a decentralised exchange where anyone can trade without the company's involvement.
* The company does not provide crypto-asset services (custody, exchange, transfer, advice, portfolio management) and therefore is not a crypto-asset service provider under MiCA.
* The 2022 NFT collection consists of unique, non-fungible passes, which MiCA generally excludes from its scope.

The company keeps this assessment under review with its legal advisers and will update this page if its activities or the regulators' guidance change.

## Services the company chose not to offer

After the meeting with the CMVM and on the advice of its legal and financial advisers, the company decided not to launch the services it had planned that would require an investment firm licence (Decree-Law no. 109-H/2021) or a virtual asset service provider registration: an exchange, custody, a digital bank, LCR purchases with card directly from the company, and consulting of a kind that constitutes investment advice. The cost of a second regulated company was not justified. If the project ever needs those services, they will be set up in a separate, licensed entity.
