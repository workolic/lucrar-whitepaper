## The project in one paragraph

Lucrar.pt is a Portuguese platform for personal finance and investing education, built on passive income. The company behind it earns rent from housing (a building with 40 rented rooms), publishes its results every quarter, and uses the platform to teach: free videos and articles, calculators and tools, a members area on Patreon, courses, and a financial game, Aldeia Lucrar, where people learn by answering questions. LCR is the utility token of that platform: it opens content and features, and members earn it by playing and by taking part in monthly challenges. It gives no right to income, no vote in the company and no claim on any asset.
