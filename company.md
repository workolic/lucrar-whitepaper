Organisation of events, fairs and exhibitions. Complementary services for businesses, outsourcing. Rental and assignment of spaces and equipment.

## Office

The office is in the BlueBiz industrial park in Setúbal, with guarded gates and surveillance. Visits are by appointment: write to workolic@lucrar.pt with the topic and reason for the meeting. The park has a meeting room the company can rent for events and there is parking next to the office.

## Share capital and the token

More than 50% of the share capital (over EUR 25,000) went to the investment rubric and from there to the LCR liquidity pool on 15 March 2022, after the audit and before launch. LCR does not represent the share capital and gives no shareholding: the CMVM asked that this be stated clearly, and the reference to "shareholders" was removed from the 2022 documents for that reason.

## Where the income comes from and where it goes

The company's income comes mainly from the rents of the housing it operates (a building with 40 rented rooms), and also from the members area on Patreon, the courses, consulting sessions and the other activities of the platform. After common expenses, the results are allocated to rubrics: the financial education fund (10%), the reserve, research and development, investment, marketing and cash. The reference percentages are on the company information page and can change whenever necessary. From December 2026 the quarterly financial statements on the homepage are the place to check what was actually done.
