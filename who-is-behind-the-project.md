One project, one owner, and a name on every analysis. Lucrar.pt belongs to Cripteracia, Unipessoal, Lda., and Cripteracia belongs to Gustavo Gomes Araújo. There are no partners, no editorial committee and no collective name signing the analyses. This page says who answers for it, with what training and under which registrations.
