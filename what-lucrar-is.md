# What Lucrar is

Lucrar.pt is a Portuguese platform for personal finance and investing education, built on passive income. The idea is simple: the company earns recurring income from housing it rents out, publishes what it earns, and spends its effort teaching people to manage and invest their own money. Financial education is the foundation for avoiding mistakes and reaching financial goals, and the project tries to deliver it by as many methods as possible.

The project started in 2018 as a YouTube channel ("Workolic") and a free course on how to earn and invest without starting capital. In 2021 it became a company, Cripteracia, Unipessoal, Lda. In 2022 it launched a utility token, LCR, and an NFT collection. Since 2023 the company has bought and built housing. In 2026 the platform gained a members area, courses, an access portal and a financial game, all tied to the same account and the same token.

## The mission

The initiative began with a petition to teach financial education in Portuguese public schools. It did not reach enough signatures. The company took the mission on itself: 10% of its results, after common expenses, go to a fund to hire financial educators and teach a curriculum to young people and adults. The percentage can change whenever necessary and is published on the company information page.

## What the platform offers

**Free education.** More than 400 free videos on the Workolic channel, followed by more than 170,000 people, organised by method: early retirement, dividends, crypto, market news, ETFs, REITs, emerging markets, companies, personal finance and real estate. On the website: financial articles written to a published editorial policy, calculators (net salary, emergency fund, effort rate, mortgage, personal credit, inflation, compound interest, FIRE, PPR, fund fees, capital gains tax and more), broker comparisons written to a neutrality rule, and tools.

**The game.** Aldeia Lucrar is a village where you learn by answering questions on stocks, crypto, personal finance and funds. It runs in the browser, it is free, and it is the front door of the platform. See "Aldeia Lucrar: the game".

**Members area (Patreon).** Three paid plans that unlock files, spreadsheets, calculators and private groups, a step-by-step learning path, the game linked to the member's account, and a monthly challenge rewarded in LCR. See "How it all connects".

**Courses.** Paid courses hosted on lucrar.pt, opened with the buyer's email. The first is "AI applied to advanced investing".

**Housing and rents.** The company operates housing that it rents out. A building with 40 rooms, rented, is its main source of recurring income; that income goes into the company's results, which are published every quarter from December 2026. It also completed one apartment in Setúbal (2025) and holds land for six apartments in Montijo. See "Real estate and rents".

**The token.** LCR opens content and features on the platform (folders of off-plan projects, the transaction register, events) and is the reward currency of the members area. See "Utility of LCR".

**Community and events.** A community of more than 1,100 paying members, individual consulting sessions with a CMVM-registered analyst, and public events (the first, for NFT holders, took place in 2023).

## What Lucrar is not

* It is not an investment firm and it does not provide investment services. The CMVM registration is personal, held by the founder as an independent financial analyst.
* It does not hold custody of anyone's crypto assets. The website never stores tokens; wallets are only read to check balances.
* It does not sell LCR to the public and has never done so. There was no pre-sale, private sale or public sale.
* It does not promise returns. Nothing on the platform is personalised financial advice.
