# Aldeia Lucrar: the game

Aldeia Lucrar ("Lucrar Village") is the project's financial game. It opens from the homepage of lucrar.pt and from the members area, runs in any browser without installing anything, and is free. It replaced the 2022 quiz game, which was a 50 MB download built on a Unity template and asked for a wallet before the first question; the village asks for nothing and lets you play first.

## How it is played

The village is a small isometric world seen from above. You walk your character through it; each zone of the village is a theme, so walking into a zone is choosing what you want to learn:

* **Bolsa (stock exchange)**: stocks and companies.
* **Praça Cripto (crypto square)**: crypto and blockchain.
* **Banco (bank)**: personal finance.
* **Mercado (market)**: funds and ETFs.

In September 2026 the village was rebuilt around a central courtyard with a fountain, with two streets crossing it, the buildings you can enter in a ring around the courtyard, and scenery houses and trees only on the outer edge. Clicking a building opens what that building does. The four industrial pipes of the old layout are gone; the rent now rises out of the chimneys and fades.

In each zone you answer multiple-choice questions. After every answer the game explains why the correct answer is correct, in one or two lines. That explanation is the point: this is a financial literacy game, not a trivia game. The bank of questions has around 600 questions in Portuguese and 600 in English, reviewed and classified by difficulty, and grows over time.

## Progress

* **Levels and characters.** Answering correctly gives "intelligence" points. Your character grows through five visual stages — young, adult, veteran, master and legend — as you level up: the master gains a skirt and a simple crown, the legend gains a cape, an ermine collar and a bigger crown. There are eight characters, four available from the start and four unlocked as you climb.
* **Five floors.** The village is the first floor. Below it are Raízes, Cavernas de Cristal, A Forja and O Núcleo, each with its own look, its own inhabitants and its own zones, unlocked at levels 8, 18, 30 and 45. The level cap is 50.
* **Energy.** Each question costs one unit of energy. Energy refills every night, starts at 18 units and grows with your level up to 40. Daily challenges and duels cost no energy.
* **The School.** From level 2, a round made only of the questions you got wrong. The best way to learn is to see your mistakes again.
* **Daily challenge.** Ten questions, the same for everyone that day, once a day.
* **Weekly board.** A ranking that adds up points since Monday, for players who have chosen a name.
* **Duels.** You can challenge a friend by sending a link; both answer the same questions and compare scores.
* **Accounts.** Progress is saved to an email account, so it follows you from phone to computer. From level 3 the game asks for an email and says why: to keep your progress and your INT, the name the game gives to experience points. Someone who played without an email and then signs in with one that already exists no longer loses anything: the experience and the games played are added together, the best result and the best streak are kept, and the prizes and links move to the email account.

## Statuses

The village has five statuses, one per door into the project. Each is a different card and each is tied to something real:

| Status | Who has it | What it looks like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| King | the manager | fixed golden clothes and a wide red mantle, a five-point crown with three stones, a sceptre, epaulettes, a medal, and the only golden aura in the game |
| Investor | anyone with a CAEP contract | the legend stage, with a green aura |
| Joker | anyone taking the AI course | a three-point hat with bells |
| Jack | a Patreon patron on a paid plan | a beret with a feather |
| Queen | a student on any course | a light three-lobed tiara |

The hierarchy is read by size, which falls in that order. Each status enters at a minimum stage: King and Investor at legend (level 45), Joker at 30, Jack at 20, Queen at 10. Experience keeps counting and rising after that, so the status is a permanent privilege and not the end of the line. Nobody ever drops a card: an investor who later buys a course stays an Investor. That rule lives in the database, not only on the screen.

**The statuses give no advantage in the game. No points, no energy, no LCR.** They are appearance only, and they are meant to stay that way: this is not a game where paying wins.

A status is only written to an account that has an email attached. Without an email the skin does not stick. That is deliberate: it keeps the card with the person instead of with the browser it was played in, and it stops someone else on the same computer picking it up. The free Patreon plan does not give a personalised character.

## The game and the token

The game is free and never asks for a wallet. LCR enters in one place only: members of the Patreon plans who play through the members area earn LCR for their progress, up to a monthly cap, and the monthly challenge awards LCR to its winner. The members area shows the member's character, level, LCR balance and statement side by side. Nothing in the game sends a blockchain transaction. The Mercado also has a counter where LCR is exchanged for discount codes on the Patreon plan; it keeps no accounting of its own and runs over the same balance as the members area (see Utility of LCR).

## What is next for the game

The order of work is: first what works with one player, then what needs many. On the list: explanations for every one of the older questions, more questions written from the founder's own material after his review, weekly leagues (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Diamond) that reset on Monday, more game modes (against the clock, survival), a home of your own inside the village, and, when there are enough players online at the same time, seeing other players move and live duels. Any new way of earning LCR in the game is announced before it exists.
