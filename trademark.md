# Trademark

The brand "LUCRAR" is registered with the Portuguese National Institute of Industrial Property (INPI), registration no. 658354, class 41 (education; training). The concession order was published in the Industrial Property Bulletin on 30 April 2021.

The registration protects the content of lucrar.pt (especially the courses), the Workolic YouTube channel, the Facebook group "Lucrar Sem Investir" and the project's other social media. The Lucrar logotype is registered.

The registration title, the logotype registration and the submission letter are published on [lucrar.pt/pt/informacoes-da-empresa](https://lucrar.pt/pt/informacoes-da-empresa/).
