What is planned, in the order the company expects to deliver it. No dates are promised beyond the ones written here. Anything not on this list is not scheduled.

## Next

* **December 2026**: first quarterly financial statements (balance sheet, income statement, cash-flow statement) published on the homepage. Then every quarter.
* **Montijo, six apartments**: approval of the updated design and specialities, then construction. Milestones and amounts are published on the project page as they happen.
* **Off-plan folders**: one folder per month continues, for holders of 1,000 LCR.
* **Aldeia Lucrar**: explanations for every question, more questions, weekly leagues, more game modes, a home of your own in the village, and live play when there are enough players. Any new way of earning LCR in the game is announced before it exists.
* **Members area and courses**: more courses and more materials per plan, announced on the portal.
* **Lucrar Pass NFTs**: new uses for existing holders inside the platform (course and community discounts, use in the games). Announced when defined.
* **The on-chain economy described in Tokenomics**: the payment contract, the Aldeia pass, the course contract, the prize contract and the tournament contract. First on the BSC testnet, then an external review, and only then the main network. No date is promised.
* **Further purchases of LCR** by the company, at its discretion, whenever results allow. Each one goes into the transaction register.

## Not planned

* No sale of LCR by the company, ever, from the main wallet.
* No new edition of the 2022 Lucrar Pass collection, and no NFT sold as an investment. The Aldeia pass and the village characters described in Tokenomics are access and game items, not a collection for sale.
* No staking, interest, burn or mint.
* No custody, exchange or transfer services by the company. The swap on the website is a non-custodial interface to PancakeSwap.
* Listing on centralised exchanges is not scheduled. The objectives of 2025 (daily volume targets, applications to OKX, KuCoin, Bybit and Binance Launchpool) are no longer current.
